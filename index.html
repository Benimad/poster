AutoPoster

A desktop app that schedules and publishes your videos to your own social media accounts, and shows how they perform.

What it does

PublishUpload one or more videos, choose which of your accounts they go to, and post now, at a set time, or from a queue.
Connect your accountsYou sign in to each platform (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X, Pinterest) through its own official login screen. AutoPoster never sees your password.
Track resultsA private dashboard shows your followers, views, likes, comments and shares across your accounts.

How it works with TikTok

  • Login Kit connects your TikTok account and reads your display name (user.info.basic).
  • Content Posting API uploads a video you choose and posts it to your own account (video.publish).
  • Your follower count and the statistics of videos posted through the app are read for your dashboard (user.info.stats, video.list).

Your data stays with you

AutoPoster runs on your own computer. Your videos, access tokens and statistics are stored only on that computer. There is no AutoPoster server and no account to create. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
