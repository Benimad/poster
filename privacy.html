Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

AutoPoster is a desktop application that you install and run on your own computer. It lets you publish videos to your own social media accounts and view statistics about them. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

Short version

Everything AutoPoster handles stays on your computer. There is no AutoPoster server, no AutoPoster account, no advertising and no analytics. We do not receive, collect, sell or share your information.

TikTok

When you connect TikTok, the app asks for these permissions through TikTok's official login screen:

  • user.info.basic: your display name and open ID.
  • user.info.stats: your follower and video counts.
  • video.publish: to post a video that you choose to your account.
  • video.list: to read statistics of your videos.

This data is used only to provide those features to you. It is never sent to anyone other than TikTok, never sold, and never used for advertising or to train models. The app never sees your TikTok password.

Sharing

The app communicates directly from your computer with the official API of each platform you connect, so your content and tokens are exchanged with those platforms only. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it. No information is sent to us or to any other third party.

Your control and deletion

  • Remove an account in the app (Accounts → Remove) to delete its stored tokens and statistics.
  • Delete the app's data folder to erase everything it stored.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings (connected apps and services), or the equivalent page of each other platform.

Children

AutoPoster is not directed to children under 13, and you must meet each platform's minimum age to connect an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
